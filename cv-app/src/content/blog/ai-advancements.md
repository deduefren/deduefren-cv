# AI Advancements

Recent AI improvements are making everyday software delivery faster and more consistent.

## Impact on Delivery

- Better code review assistance for finding common regressions.
- Faster onboarding through generated explanations and examples.
- More effective documentation and release-note generation.

## Engineering Considerations

- Keep human approval in the loop for critical changes.
- Align outputs with architecture standards and coding conventions.
- Continuously verify generated behavior with automated tests.

AI is most useful when integrated into existing engineering discipline rather than replacing it.
